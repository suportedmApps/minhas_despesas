# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

## Introduction

Minhas Despesas ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Minhas Despesas application.

Minhas Despesas is provided free of charge and is intended to be used as provided.

The App allows you to record, organize, and manage personal expenses, track payments, monitor recurring expenses, and view financial information to help you manage your budget.

The App is designed to store information generated through your use of the App locally on your device, without requiring an account or registration.

By using the App, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information Storage

Minhas Despesas is designed to store information generated through your use of the App locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use, information stored locally may include:

* Expense names and descriptions
* Expense amounts and due dates
* Expense categories and types
* Payment records and payment dates
* Recurring expense settings
* Contract information and expense duration settings
* Financial summaries and other information generated by the App
* App preferences and settings

Your expenses, payment records, and other information entered into the App are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

The App is designed to allow you to access your saved financial information without requiring an Internet connection for its core functionality.

No user account or registration is required to use the App.

The developer does not have direct access to information stored locally within the App.

---

## Personal Information

Minhas Despesas does not require you to provide personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Home address
* Government identification
* Bank account credentials

The App does not require an account to access its core features.

Financial information that you enter or generate while using the App is stored locally on your device.

The developer does not independently collect or maintain this information on its own servers.

You should avoid entering sensitive personal information into expense descriptions or other fields unless necessary for your own use.

---

## Expenses and User-Generated Content

Minhas Despesas allows you to create, edit, organize, and manage expense records on your device.

User-generated content may include:

* Expense names and descriptions
* Expense amounts
* Expense types and categories
* Due dates and payment dates
* Recurring expense information
* Monthly or yearly contract settings
* Expense duration and period settings
* Payment status and payment history
* Other information voluntarily entered into the App

This information is used solely to provide the App's expense-management, payment-tracking, and financial-organization functionality.

Expenses, payment records, and other user-generated content are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to or maintained on servers operated by the developer.

If the App is uninstalled or its local storage is cleared, locally stored expenses, payment records, and related information may be deleted.

## Notifications

Minhas Despesas may use notifications to remind you about upcoming payments or other events related to your expenses, depending on the features you enable.

Notifications are intended to provide functionality requested or configured by the user within the App.

The App does not require you to provide personal information to use its notification functionality.

Information required to schedule local notifications may be stored or managed locally on your device.

The App does not send your expense records or payment history to the developer's servers in order to provide local notifications.

If notifications are disabled through the App or your device settings, some reminder functionality may no longer be available.

## Data Deletion

Minhas Despesas stores user-generated information, including expense records, payment history, recurring expense settings, and related information, locally on the user's device.

Because the developer does not maintain a server-side account or database containing this information, the developer cannot directly delete information stored locally on your device.

If you wish to delete the information stored by Minhas Despesas, you can:

* Use any data-clearing or deletion functionality provided within the App, if available;
* Delete individual expenses or payment records through the App, where these functionalities are available;
* Clear the App's storage/data through your Android device settings; or
* Uninstall Minhas Despesas from your device.

These actions remove locally stored App data associated with the installation, depending on the action performed.

Please note that information processed independently by third-party services, such as Google AdMob, is subject to the respective provider's privacy policies and available privacy controls.

If you have questions about information processed by a third-party service, you should contact the relevant service provider directly.

Users should be aware that anyone who gains access to an unlocked device may also be able to access information stored in the App.
